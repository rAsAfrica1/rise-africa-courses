📋 Poultry Housing Requirements

Different bird types need different amounts of space and housing specifications:

Poultry Type Floor Space (per bird) Roost Space Nesting Boxes Recommended Housing Style
Broiler Chickens 0.5-0.7 m² 15 cm per bird 1 box per 4-5 birds Deep litter system or cage
Layers (Hens) 0.7-1.0 m² 20-25 cm per bird 1 box per 2-3 birds Battery cage or aviary system
Turkeys 1.5-2.0 m² 40 cm per bird 1 box per 3-4 birds Shed with roosting bars
Ducks 1.0-1.5 m² 25-30 cm per bird 1 box per 2-3 birds Elevated coop with water access
Geese 2.0-2.5 m² 40-50 cm per bird 1 box per 2-3 birds Shed with pasture access
Guinea Fowl 0.7-0.9 m² 20 cm per bird 1 box per 3 birds Shed with elevated roosting

🏗️ Housing Specifications

Essential Housing Features:

Feature Requirement
Ventilation 30-50% of wall area for air circulation; prevents heat, ammonia, moisture
Lighting Natural: 1 window per 30 m²; Artificial: 10-15 lux minimum for 14-16 hours/day
Flooring 10-15 cm litter depth (wood shavings, straw); elevated for waterfowl
Temperature Control Chicks: 32-35°C; Adults: 18-24°C; adequate insulation/ventilation
Perches/Roosting Bars Height: 60-100 cm; 5-8 cm diameter poles; spaced 25-30 cm apart
Nesting Boxes 25×30×40 cm (H×W×D); dark, quiet, private; lined with soft material
Water Systems Automatic drinkers or manual troughs; 1 meter of drinking space per 100 birds
Feeding Systems 4-5 cm feeder space per bird; feeders 10-15 cm above litter; protected from waste
Drainage Sloped flooring (1:100); gutters; proper waste management system
Fencing/Security 1.5-2.0 m height; buried 30 cm to prevent predator entry; lockable gates

⚙️ Implementation Steps

STEP 1: Assess Your Space & Climate
Evaluate location, size, and environmental conditions
What to do:
  • Measure your available land/building space
  • Document your climate zone (tropical, temperate, arid, highland)
  • Identify prevailing winds and sun exposure
  • Check drainage and water availability
  • Note proximity to neighbors and water sources

Expected output: Site assessment document with dimensions, climate data, photos

Time needed: 2-3 hours

STEP 2: Determine Housing Type & Design
Select appropriate housing system for your birds and climate
What to do:
  • Decide on housing type (deep litter, battery cage, aviary, free-range, etc.)
  • Sketch your housing design on paper
  • Calculate total floor space needed (bird count × space requirement)
  • Plan ventilation and lighting arrangements
  • Research traditional/local building methods that work in your area

Expected output: Detailed housing design sketch with measurements

Time needed: 3-4 hours

STEP 3: Calculate Space Requirements
Determine exact dimensions based on flock size
What to do:
  • Multiply expected bird count by space per bird requirement
  • Calculate roost/perch space needed
  • Determine nesting box quantity
  • Plan feeder and water system dimensions
  • Document all calculations with formulas

Expected output: Space calculations spreadsheet with all dimensions

Time needed: 1-2 hours

STEP 4: List Building Materials & Equipment
Identify all materials needed for construction
What to do:
  • List structural materials (poles, wood, metal frame, etc.)
  • Identify roofing materials with insulation properties
  • Plan fencing materials and hardware
  • List equipment needed (feeders, drinkers, perches, nesting boxes)
  • Include bedding materials (wood shavings, straw, sand)
  • Note ventilation components and lighting fixtures

Expected output: Complete materials list with quantities and local suppliers

Time needed: 2-3 hours

STEP 5: Obtain Cost Quotes & Develop Budget
Research prices and create construction budget
What to do:
  • Visit local hardware stores and suppliers
  • Get price quotes for all materials
  • Calculate labor costs (hire builders or DIY)
  • Add 10-15% contingency for unexpected costs
  • Create detailed construction budget breakdown
  • Calculate cost per bird for comparison

Expected output: Detailed budget sheet with material costs, labor, and total investment

Time needed: 3-4 hours

STEP 6: Plan Implementation Timeline
Create construction and setup schedule
What to do:
  • Create month-by-month construction timeline
  • Identify optimal construction season (weather considerations)
  • Plan material procurement schedule
  • Schedule labor if hiring workers
  • Plan equipment installation and testing before birds arrive
  • Build in buffer time for delays

Expected output: 12-month implementation timeline with key milestones

Time needed: 2-3 hours

🎯 Real-World Project: Housing Design & Budget Plan

Create a complete poultry housing design plan for your actual farm operation.

Project Deliverables (Submit as PDF or Word):

1. Site Assessment Report

1 page including: land dimensions, climate zone, photos of proposed location, drainage analysis

2. Housing Design Sketch

Scale drawing with dimensions showing: floor plan, side elevation, ventilation placement, roof design

3. Space Calculation Document

Mathematical calculations for: total floor space, roost space, nesting boxes, feeder/drinker requirements

4. Materials & Equipment List

Comprehensive list with quantities: structural materials, roofing, fencing, equipment, bedding supplies

5. Construction Budget

Detailed breakdown: materials costs, labor costs, equipment, contingency (10-15%), total investment, cost per bird

6. 12-Month Implementation Timeline

Month-by-month schedule: material procurement, construction phases, equipment installation, occupancy date

7. Climate Adaptation Plan

How your design addresses: seasonal temperature changes, rainfall, wind, seasonal income/expense variations

8. Photos & Documentation

Photos of site, reference images, local building examples, sketches of design details

Total length: 8-12 pages with photos

Grading criteria:

  • Completeness (20%): All 8 deliverables included
  • Realism (20%): Design is feasible in your context with available resources
  • Accuracy (20%): Calculations are correct, costs reflect actual local prices
  • Clarity (20%): Well-organized, easy to follow, photos are clear
  • Detail (20%): Thorough research, specific to your location
