## 🔧 IMPLEMENTATION STEPS (Farm Work)

These are NOT classroom exercises. You do these ON YOUR FARM.

### **How They Work:**

Each module has 6 implementation steps. Example from Module 1:

```
STEP 1: Research Pig Farming in Your Region
What to do:
  - Contact agricultural extension office
  - Ask about climate suitability
  - Interview 3 local pig farmers
  - Document: climate data + farmer interviews

Expected output: Notes from 3 interviews + climate information
Time needed: 2-3 hours
```

### **Tips for Success:**

✅ Do steps in order (each builds on the previous)
✅ Take photos/screenshots of your work
✅ Write down measurements and observations
✅ Talk to farmers - they're your best resource
✅ Document everything (you'll need it for the project)

### **Where to Find Help:**

- **YouTube:** Search for your specific question
- **Extension office:** Free advice from agriculture experts
- **Local farmers:** Visit neighbors doing pig farming
- **Your instructor:** Email if stuck (response within 24 hours)

## 📝 TAKING THE QUIZ

Each module has a 3-question quiz. Here's how it works:

### **Quiz Rules:**
- Multiple choice (4 options each)
- You need 2/3 (66%) to PASS
- You can take it unlimited times
- Answers are revealed after you submit
- Not a barrier - it's for learning

### **How to Take Quiz:**

1. **Read the question carefully**
   - Don't rush
   - Understand what's being asked

2. **Select your answer**
   - Click the radio button (circle)
   - Only one answer per question

3. **Click "Submit Quiz"**
   - See your score immediately
   - Green box = correct answer
   - Read the explanation

4. **If you fail:**
   - Read the feedback
   - Review the video or implementation step
   - Clear answers and try again
   - No penalty for retrying

### **Quiz Strategy:**

✅ Watch all videos first
✅ Complete implementation steps
✅ Review before taking quiz
✅ Take your time
✅ Learn from mistakes

## 🆘 TROUBLESHOOTING

### **"Video Won't Load"**
- Check internet connection
- Refresh the page
- Try different browser
- Videos need internet to load from YouTube

### **"Progress Not Saving"**
- Check browser settings (localStorage may be off)
- Try different browser
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Manually save important notes to file

### **"Can't Open HTML File"**
- Make sure it ends in `.html`
- Double-click the file
- Or drag it to your browser window
- If still stuck, ask instructor

### **"I'm Stuck on Implementation Step"**
- Re-watch the related video
- Call local agricultural extension office
- Visit a farmer doing the same activity
- Email instructor with photos of your situation
- Ask in discussion forum (if available)

### **"Question About the Content"**
- Check the video again (answer usually there)
- Search YouTube for the specific topic
- Contact instructor (within 24 hours)
- Don't wait - ask questions!

### **"Can't Submit Project"**
- Save as PDF first (then upload)
- File size should be <10 MB
- Attach photos separately if needed
- Email instructor with filename and module number
- Keep a copy for yourself
