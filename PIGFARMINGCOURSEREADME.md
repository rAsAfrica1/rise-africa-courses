## TROUBLESHOOTING

### **Q: Videos not loading?**
A: Ensure internet connection. YouTube embeds require live internet. Or download videos locally and edit embed URLs.

### **Q: Progress not saving?**
A: Check browser settings - localStorage may be disabled. Ensure cookies/local storage allowed. Works in all major browsers.

### **Q: Can't open HTML files?**
A: Ensure file extension is .html. Double-click to open in default browser. Or drag file to browser window.

### **Q: Content in wrong language?**
A: Edit HTML files - search for English text, replace with your language.

### **Q: Videos in wrong language?**
A: Use video-curation-guide.txt to find videos in your language. Update embed URLs in module HTML.
