📋 6 Implementation Steps

Step 1: Choose Rabbit Breed

New Zealand White or Californian: BEST for meat production. Fast growth (8-10 weeks), good feed conversion, docile. Cost: $15-30 per rabbit. Start with 1 buck, 2-3 does.

Step 2: Build Simple Housing

Cages: $100-300 for 3-4 animals. Each 60cm×60cm×45cm. Metal frame, wire mesh, solid floor. Roofed shelter for protection. Location: shaded, good ventilation, predator-proof.

Step 3: Source Quality Feed

Rabbits eat: hay/grass (free), vegetables, commercial pellets ($0.30-0.50/kg). Daily cost: $0.30-0.50 per rabbit. Water: constant access (1-2 liters/rabbit/day). Total monthly: $30-50 for 3 rabbits.

Step 4: Establish Breeding Schedule

Does breed year-round. Gestation: 31 days. Litter size: 8-10. Weaning: 4 weeks. Production cycle: Every 5-6 weeks one doe produces 8-10 marketable rabbits. From 3 does: 20-30 rabbits/month!

Step 5: Learn Health & Breeding Management

Prevent: VRFD (viral), snuffles (bacterial), external parasites. Keep detailed records: breeding dates, litter size, weight gain, mortality. This data increases profitability and reputation.

Step 6: Calculate Profitability & Scale

Initial: 1 buck + 3 does + cages = $200-400. Monthly production: 20-30 rabbits. Revenue: 25 rabbits × 4kg × $5/kg = $500/month. Feed cost: $150. PROFIT: $350/month! Reinvest to expand.

🚀 Real-World Project: Rabbit Farm Plan

Part 1: Market Research

Survey 5 butchers/restaurants: interested in rabbit meat? Price/kg? Annual volume? Dried skins valuable?

Part 2: Housing Design

Sketch your rabbit hutch system. Cost per unit? Space for 3 + expansion capacity? Ventilation, drainage, predator protection?

Part 3: Startup Budget

Calculate: animals ($50-100), cages ($200-300), feeders/drinkers ($30-50), initial feed/hay ($50). Total: $330-500. When do you break-even?

Part 4: Breeding Schedule

Create 12-month calendar. When will you breed first does? Track litter dates, weaning, slaughter dates. Plan sales volume month-by-month.

Part 5: Feed & Nutrition

Research pellet suppliers. Cost per kg? Hay sources free/cheap? Supplement vegetables available? Monthly feed budget: ___

Part 6: Processing & Marketing

How will you slaughter/process (DIY or hire butcher)? Storage: refrigeration? Distribution: direct, restaurants, market? Packaging?

Part 7: Health & Records

Create record-keeping system: breeding dates, litter info, mortality, weights, health issues. How will you prevent disease?

Part 8: Scaling Plan

Year 1: 3 does. Year 2: ___ does? Year 3: ___? Space/feed/labor requirements? Profitability at each level?
