🛠️ Implementation Steps

  1. Select Breed: Choose 3-5 goats suited to your climate
  2. Build Shelter: Construct secure housing (200 sq ft minimum)
  3. Plan Feeding: Obtain hay, grain, and grazing land
  4. Health Management: Schedule vaccinations and check-ups
  5. Breeding Program: Plan strategic breeding schedule
  6. Market Planning: Identify buyers for milk, meat, or fiber
