# Rise Africa Skills - Complete Course Catalog

## Platform Overview

Rise Africa Skills is a comprehensive online agricultural education platform designed to empower African farmers with practical, profit-focused knowledge. The platform consists of 12 flagship agricultural courses, each covering a complete commodity value chain from basics to business planning.

### Choose based on:
1. **Market Demand** - What will farmers in your region buy?
2. **Startup Capital** - Low: Poultry, Rabbit, Mushroom; Medium: Fish, Beekeeping; High: Cattle, Orchard
3. **Learning Curve** - Easy: Pig, Poultry; Moderate: Beekeeping, Feed Making; Complex: Orchard, Aquaculture
4. **Time to Profit** - Fast: Poultry (weeks), Mushroom (weeks); Medium: Pig (months), Rabbit (months); Long: Tree, Cattle (years)
5. **Land Requirements** - Minimal: Beekeeping, Mushroom, Rabbit; Moderate: Fish, Feed Mill; Extensive: Forestry, Cattle, Orchard
6. **Environmental Factors** - Consider rainfall, temperature, altitude, soil type for each region
