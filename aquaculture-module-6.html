🛠️ Implementation Steps

  1. Site Selection: Choose location with water and sunlight
  2. Pond Construction: Dig and line pond (at least 1.5m deep)
  3. Water Preparation: Fill and test water quality
  4. Stock Selection: Select 500-1000 fingerlings per 1000m²
  5. Feeding Program: Establish daily feeding schedule
  6. Monitoring: Test water daily, track fish growth
