📋 Poultry Nutritional Requirements

Different bird types and growth stages have different nutritional needs:

Bird Type & Stage Daily Feed (g/bird) Protein (%) Energy (kcal/kg) Calcium (%)
Chicks (0-4 weeks) 15-25 22-24% 2,800-3,000 0.9-1.0%
Growers (5-12 weeks) 40-80 18-20% 2,700-2,900 0.8-0.9%
Broilers (finishing) 100-130 20-22% 3,000-3,100 0.8-0.9%
Layers (in production) 90-120 16-18% 2,700-2,900 3.5-4.0%
Turkeys (growers) 60-100 24-26% 2,900-3,100 1.0-1.2%
Ducks (layers) 120-150 15-17% 2,900-3,100 3.0-3.5%

⚙️ Implementation Steps

STEP 1: Research Local Feed Availability
Identify feed sources in your region
What to do:
  • List all available feed ingredients in your area (grains, legumes, by-products)
  • Visit local grain merchants and feed suppliers
  • Ask farmers what they feed their birds
  • Identify agricultural by-products (mill waste, vegetable scraps, etc.)
  • Document seasonal availability of ingredients

Expected output: List of available feed ingredients with seasonal availability notes

Time needed: 2-3 hours

STEP 2: Calculate Flock Feed Requirements
Determine total daily and monthly feed needs
What to do:
  • List each bird type and age group in your flock
  • Multiply bird count × daily feed requirement per bird
  • Calculate total daily feed needs (all birds combined)
  • Multiply by 30 to get monthly requirements
  • Create spreadsheet with calculations for each bird type

Expected output: Detailed feed requirement calculations by bird type and month

Time needed: 2-3 hours

STEP 3: Design Balanced Feeding Rations
Create nutritionally complete feed mixtures
What to do:
  • Select 3-4 main ingredients (grain + protein sources)
  • Calculate percentage of each ingredient needed to meet nutritional targets
  • Test ration formulas to balance protein, energy, calcium
  • Create 2-3 alternative rations using different local ingredients
  • Document ration formulas with exact percentages and costs

Expected output: 2-3 balanced feed rations with ingredient percentages and nutritional targets met

Time needed: 3-4 hours

STEP 4: Get Feed Ingredient Prices
Research current market prices for ingredients
What to do:
  • Get current prices from 3+ local suppliers for each ingredient
  • Calculate cost per kilogram of each ingredient
  • Identify bulk pricing and seasonal price variations
  • Compare cost of homemade rations vs. commercial feed
  • Document prices and supplier contact information

Expected output: Feed price list with supplier information and cost comparisons

Time needed: 3-4 hours

STEP 5: Develop Feeding & Management System
Plan how you'll feed and manage your flock daily
What to do:
  • Decide feeding frequency (1x, 2x, or free-choice per day)
  • Plan feeding schedule that fits your farm work routine
  • Calculate feeding equipment needed (feeders, drinkers, storage)
  • Plan storage system for feed (pest-proof, dry conditions)
  • Document daily and weekly feeding procedures
  • Plan for water management (continuous, scheduled, or wet feeding)

Expected output: Feeding management plan with daily procedures, equipment needs, and storage solution

Time needed: 2-3 hours

STEP 6: Create Annual Feed Budget
Project total feed costs for 12 months
What to do:
  • Calculate monthly feed costs for each bird group
  • Account for seasonal price variations
  • Add 10-15% contingency for price increases
  • Calculate feed cost per kg of meat/eggs produced
  • Project annual budget and identify high-cost months
  • Plan for cash flow challenges in expensive months

Expected output: 12-month feed budget with monthly costs, contingency, and cost-per-product analysis

Time needed: 2-3 hours
