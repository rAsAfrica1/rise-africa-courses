🏠 Housing Design Specifications

Space Requirements (per pig)

🏢 Piglets (newly weaned, 10-25 kg)

Space needed: 0.4-0.5 m² per piglet inside, 1.0-1.5 m² outside pen

Housing type: Draft-free shelter with heat lamp in cool seasons

🐷 Growers (25-60 kg)

Space needed: 0.6-0.8 m² per pig inside, 1.5-2.0 m² outside pen

Housing type: Ventilated shed with wallows to cool off in heat

🐗 Finishers (60-110 kg)

Space needed: 0.8-1.0 m² per pig inside, 2.0-2.5 m² outside pen

Housing type: Sturdy shelter with exercise space, wallows for cooling

👩‍🌾 Breeding Sows

Space needed: 4-6 m² (gestation), 7-8 m² (farrowing pen)

Housing type: Comfortable shelter with nesting area, separate farrowing pen

Essential Housing Features

✓ Shelter & Shade

Protects from sun, rain, and wind. Corrugated metal, thatched roof, or timber construction. Shade is critical in hot climates to prevent stress and heat stroke.

✓ Ventilation

Good air flow prevents ammonia buildup and respiratory disease. Open sided construction works in many African contexts. Avoid completely enclosed sheds without ventilation.

✓ Wallows

Pigs regulate body temperature by wallowing in mud/water. Provide wallows or spray systems in hot climates. A simple mud pit or water trough works well.

✓ Drainage

Manure and urine must drain away. Sloped floors or elevated slatted areas prevent pigs standing in filth. Drainage reduces disease and improves hygiene.

✓ Fencing & Safety

Strong fencing prevents escapes and protects from predators. Pigs are strong diggers - bury fencing 30cm deep. Use 1.0-1.2m height for grower/finisher pigs.

✓ Water & Feeding Areas

Clean water access 24/7. Feeding troughs prevent waste. Separate feeding area keeps housing cleaner and reduces competition.

🔧 Implementation Steps

Complete these 6 steps to finalize your breed choice and design your housing:

Week 2-3 Action Items

1
Compare 3-4 Pig Breeds for Your Context
  • Visit farms raising different breeds in your region
  • Interview farmers about: breed performance, feed costs, market demand, disease issues
  • Create comparison table of 3 breeds (use the chart above as template)
  • Consider your climate, markets, and budget constraints
  • Make a final breed selection with detailed reasoning
2
Find Local Breeding Stock Sources
  • Locate 2-3 reputable breeders selling your chosen breed
  • Get pricing for breeding sows and boars
  • Verify health status and breed purity
  • Ask about genetics, growth performance, and offspring records
  • Document contact info and availability timeline
3
Measure Your Site & Plan Housing Layout
  • Mark out your proposed pig area on your property
  • Take measurements: length, width, slope for drainage
  • Note: water source location, wind direction, proximity to neighbors
  • Draw a scale diagram showing: shelter location, feeding area, wallow, water source, access paths
  • Identify potential problems (drainage, predators, neighbor complaints)
4
Calculate Space Requirements & Herd Size
  • Determine your target herd size based on available space
  • Calculate total shelter space needed (use space requirements above)
  • Calculate total pen space (inside + outside)
  • Example: 10 grower pigs need 8-10 m² inside shelter + 20-25 m² outside pen
  • Verify you have adequate space and adjust herd size if needed
5
Design Your Housing System
  • Choose housing design based on your climate and budget (see examples above)
  • Create detailed sketch showing: shelter layout, pen divisions, feeding areas, wallows
  • Ensure design includes: ventilation, drainage, water access, predator protection
  • Get feedback from experienced farmers - ask if design will work
  • Take photos or show design to locals for suggestions
6
Create Detailed Construction Plan & Budget
  • List all materials needed: wood, metal roofing, fencing, gates, feeders, water systems
  • Get quotes from local suppliers for each material
  • Calculate labor costs (DIY vs. hiring)
  • Create itemized budget for construction
  • Identify where to source materials and schedule construction timeline

📝 Knowledge Check Quiz

Test your understanding with these 3 questions:

Question 1: How much space does a 50 kg grower pig need inside a shelter?

Correct Answer: B - Grower pigs (25-60 kg) need 0.6-0.8 m² inside shelter to prevent crowding stress.

Question 2: Why are wallows important in hot African climates?

Correct Answer: B - Wallows are essential in hot climates for thermoregulation and reducing stress.

Question 3: Which breed is generally BEST for small African farms?

Correct Answer: A - Kunekune and hardy crosses work best for small farms in African climates.
