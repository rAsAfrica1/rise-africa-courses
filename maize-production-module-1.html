🛠️ Implementation Steps

  1. Land Preparation: Plow and harrow 2-3 times
  2. Seed Selection: Choose certified, improved variety
  3. Planting: Space 75cm rows × 25cm plants (40kg seed/hectare)
  4. Fertilizer Application: Apply at planting and 4 weeks later
  5. Weed Control: Hand hoe at 3 and 8 weeks
  6. Harvesting: Harvest when kernels are dry and hard
